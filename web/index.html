1. OpenAI MATH Dataset Overview

The MATH benchmark consists of 12,500 high-school competition mathematical problems (AMC 10, AMC 12, AIME, USAMO) categorized into 7 sub-disciplines with difficulty standard from Level 1 to Level 5.

2. Universal Answer Normalization

Answers are explicitly enclosed inside \boxed{...} tags. The evaluation pipeline uses recursive LaTeX parsing and SymPy algebraic equivalency checks to grade model output automatically.

3. Chain-of-Thought (CoT) & Step-Decomposition

Every problem includes step-by-step human explanations. Models trained or prompted on CoT step derivation achieve vastly improved accuracy on Level 4 & 5 competition math.

4. Android Native & Web Synthesis

Mira Math AI integrates KaTeX for client-side zero-latency math rendering and Java Android native bridge for tactile haptics, clipboard integration, and native sharing.
